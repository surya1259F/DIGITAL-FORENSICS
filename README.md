---

## 🔒 Forensic Integrity Standards

- **Source of Truth**: Experiment titles, objectives, and procedure definitions reflect the official laboratory manual.
- **Empirical Evidence**: Every procedure step is directly supported by high-resolution visual evidence captured during actual laboratory execution.
- **Cryptographic Hash Verification**: MD5 and SHA-1 hashes were calculated and verified to validate bitstream integrity.
- **Scenario Transparency**: All experiments explicitly identify whether test or controlled laboratory datasets were utilized.
